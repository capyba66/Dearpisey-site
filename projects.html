Heart's Melody

Ruby Chen, the lead singer and head of her school’s music club, is in trouble. When her parents find out about her being in the music club, they insist she must quit. According to them, she should be spending more time on passing the exam that will dictate her future. Ruby couldn’t care less about the exam but regardless of how she feels, she needs to please her parents.

Meanwhile Minnie Wu has had a secret crush on Ruby ever since their freshman year of high school but now that they’re in their senior year, she can’t even talk to her! She is determined to not let this love fail like in their past life. This year, Minnie gathers the help of Ruby’s brother, Jason and with his advice, attempts to win Ruby’s heart.

Ruby doesn’t believe in fate or past lives. That is until she meets Minnie Wu who promises to help her convince her parents to let Ruby stay in the music club.

Leave This Love In Flames

Being haunted by vengeful ghost isn’t a typical reason for marriage. But to be fair, being haunted by a ghost who is out to kill you can make you go to wild lengths.

Arthit is an art major in a university and should be living the average life of a poor starving artist where his biggest worries should be his looming deadlines. Instead of looming deadlines taking over his life, he has to survive a vengeful ghost who is trying to take his life. Naturally, his parents were so desperate to undo the family curse that they decided to marry him off to his ex boyfriend.
